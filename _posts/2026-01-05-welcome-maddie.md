## Welcome Maddie!
Welcome, Maddie Hicks! We are pleased to announce that Maddie Hicks has joined the group. Currently a Master’s student at Colorado School of Mines, Maddie’s research will focus on developing bottom-up synthetic processes to design tailored microporous environments. Her work aims to precisely engineer active sites to induce confinement effects, pushing the boundaries of catalytic control. Welcome to the team, Maddie!
