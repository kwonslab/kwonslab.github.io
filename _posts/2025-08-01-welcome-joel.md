## Welcome Joel!
Welcome, Joel Orozco! We are excited to welcome Joel Orozco to the research group! Joel is currently a Master’s student at Colorado School of Mines. His research will focus on characterizing Lewis acid-base properties in metal-oxide catalysts and investigating their impact on alkanol dehydration and dehydrogenation pathways. We look forward to seeing the contributions he will make to the team!
